## Pro Tips & How-To Section for AI Agents / Automation

**LinkedIn Apply & Outreach Examples**  
- **Easy Apply**: Many jobs show an “Easy Apply” button. Agents can fill forms automatically using profile data (headline, experience, resume upload).  
- **Manual/Direct Apply**: Click through to company career page.  
- **Example Search Query** (copy-paste ready):  
  `("Senior UX Designer" OR "Product Designer" OR "Lead Product Designer" OR "Sr. UX" OR "UI/UX Designer") (Remote OR Pittsburgh) -intern`  
- Use LinkedIn Recruiter or Sales Navigator for advanced filtering and InMail outreach. Set job alerts for real-time notifications.

**Indeed Apply & API Options**  
- **Indeed Apply (One-Click)**: Thousands of jobs have the “Apply Now” button that pulls your Indeed resume. Ideal for high-volume applications.  
- **Sponsored Posts / Employer Dashboard**: Companies use Indeed’s employer tools to post jobs and receive applications directly.  
- **API / Integration**: Indeed Partner APIs (for approved partners) allow bulk job posting, application retrieval, and ATS syncing. For individual use, agents can monitor RSS feeds or use third-party tools like Zapier to trigger actions on new postings matching keywords.  
- **Example Search Setup**: Filter by “Remote” + “Senior UX Designer” + salary range, then enable daily email alerts. Use Boolean: `(UX OR "Product Design") Senior (Remote OR Pittsburgh)`.

These examples give your agents a concrete starting point for scaling applications and messaging.
